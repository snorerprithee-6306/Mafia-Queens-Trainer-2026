**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Mafia Queens**. This trainer assists with Mafia Queens match-3 stages and progression systems by providing extra puzzle flexibility, restoring failed attempts, and supporting relationship and story progression.

**Target Audience:** Adult players who want assistance with match-3 stages, relationship progression, and story exploration in Mafia Queens.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Mafia Queens is presented as an interactive comic and match-3 experience rather than a conventional character-controlled action game. Movement controls are therefore not applicable.

### Tools & Inventory

The game focuses on match-3 progression, story choices, relationships, and loyalty rewards instead of a traditional equipment inventory. Use the trainer only while progressing through supported game screens.

### World & Environment

Advance through interactive mafia comic chapters by completing match-3 boards and making story choices. Relationship decisions influence loyalty and the progression of queen-focused content.

### Combat & Weapons

Mafia Queens does not use conventional real-time combat. Match-3 boards provide the primary gameplay challenge and unlock power, danger, story progress, and mature animated scenes.
